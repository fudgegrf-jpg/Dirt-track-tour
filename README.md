# Dirt-track-tour

Mobile-ready Three.js dirt late model racing: two active tracks, your team, and the miles between.

**Play:** https://raw.githack.com/fudgegrf-jpg/Dirt-track-tour/main/index.html

## Higher detail / Smoother contacts / 25-mph corner pit road
Both active tracks now share a drivable, detailed track-integrated paddock. The 25-mph pit road peels from a corner, loops behind the oval, and enters a left-side paddock. Beveled haulers, diamond-plate ramps, pitched canopies, detailed pumps/storefront, textured lots and instanced foliage scale with quality; static truck meshes are batched and distant forest chunks are culled. Race and articulated semi collisions use bounded substep corrections and low rebound, with no collision teleporting. Results retain the same race world/car; use the marked corner gate/loop road, find Bay 42, line up and load onto the open ramp. Crew assist costs $300. Tie-down/door animations lead directly to the highway. Pace laps and cautions auto-drive by default with a smooth green-flag handover; disable in Settings. One physical midpoint fuel stop per leg, with off/on ramps, replaces the refuel menu: park in the highlighted pump bay to fill in real time, then merge back. Signed parking bays preserve rest and discounted road-service repairs without a menu. Overpass supports now sit outside lanes and the median; regional greenery uses corrected natural instance colors. J / touch Jake toggles the richer inline-six diesel engine brake. [Hear diesel demos](audio-samples/semi-demos.html).

Your default driver is **Jayden Fudge #42**, with the selectable **Classic 42** paint: off-white sides, royal-blue nose/hood/roof, yellow outlined numbers, lime rocker trim and fictional contingency decals. The default identity and Classic 42 livery remain intact; the new model adds physical tread and coachwork detail. Only untouched legacy Dale Rhodes/Jayden Fudge #21 red/white default profiles migrate; customized profiles stay yours. Migration preserves points, results, cash and crew. Edit the profile before starting a career to personalize your team and livery.

Phone: turn sideways. Steer on the left and hold GAS/BRAKE on the right. Optional tilt, camera, pause, fullscreen, cruise/exit and loading-winch controls appear for each stage. First touch unlocks audio. Phones start on Auto / Low graphics.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows drive, C camera, P/Esc pause, H help. See START-HERE.txt and CREDITS.txt.

Fonts and icons are embedded; engine/effect audio is synthesized, and owner-supplied menu music is embedded as compressed MP3 text chunks. No install/build is needed. To play locally, serve this folder over HTTP. Raw.githack can cache branch updates for a few minutes and may show an “Open the page” confirmation before the game. A commit-pinned rawcdn.githack link avoids branch-version caching.

**Reverse:** hold S/down-arrow or the touch Brake control to stop, then continue holding to back the semi at up to 2.5 m/s. ### Fast first-tap Nitro Riffs / mobile HUD 2 / Semi 2 / drivable infields

The semi engine is an original layered Web Audio synthesis of a heavy inline-six: low firing-order rumble and cylinder imbalance, load growl/injection clatter, swept turbo, lift-off air discharge, a ten-speed audio gearbox with RPM drops, service/stop air hisses, Jake bark, tire/road rumble and a dual-tone air horn. Deep infield excursions/shortcuts invalidate the next lap crossing and show a rejoin warning; R recovers to the clay without granting a cut lap.
