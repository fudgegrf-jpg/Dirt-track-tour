DIRT TRACK TOUR - credits and licenses
======================================

CODE, ART AND SOUND
  All game code, 3D models, textures, liveries and decals are made procedurally in this project's
  own code (js/). The race cars, haulers, tracks and scenery are built from code at run time.

  AUDIO: every sound is synthesized live in code with the Web Audio API (js/audio.js):
  the V8 (firing-order pulse synthesis, intake/exhaust, valvetrain and gear whine),
  tire/dirt noise, crowd, wind, and the semi's diesel and jake brake.
  No audio recordings or third-party sound assets are used for engines/effects.
  The WAV files in audio-samples/ are offline renders of that same synthesizer, made for previewing.

  SPONSORS AND BRANDS: all sponsor, contingency, tire and fuel brand names on the cars are made up
  for this game. Any resemblance to a real company is unintended. Track names refer to real US
  venues for flavor only; the game is not affiliated with or endorsed by any track or sanctioning body.

CAMMED CROSS-PLANE V8
  Original procedural combustion, cam-overlap variation, open-header resonance and turbulence synthesis.
  No engine recordings or third-party sound samples used. Musical overrun accents use the existing continuous voice graph.
  The independent procedural semi/diesel audio remains unchanged.

NIGHT SHOWS / PERFORMANCE STAGE 1
Original procedural night lighting, single-pass clay/mud shading, pooled instanced
clods and fictional contingency artwork. No new third-party art/audio or licenses.
Commercial-game photographs were private visual research only; none are shipped.

DEEPER V8 VOICING
  Longer virtual exhaust resonators, smooth pressure body and existing-node throat EQ.
  Original synthesis only; no new samples, external sources or licenses.

REFERENCE-INFORMED CHOPPED V8 IDLE
  All combustion, cam modulation, exhaust pressure and header-air texture are
  generated mathematically. Private audio reference was studied for character;
  no recording, waveform extract, impulse response or sample is embedded.
  No new third-party assets or licenses. Private review files never ship.
