Original layered Web Audio synthesis of a heavy inline-six diesel. These are test renders from the actual game engine, not real truck recordings or an exact Cummins/Detroit simulation. Nine RPM drops span ten audio gears. Headphones or a speaker that reproduces bass are recommended; a phone speaker cannot reproduce the deepest rumble.
